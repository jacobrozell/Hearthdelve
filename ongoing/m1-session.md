# M1 session — the well and one Rift

Started 2026-10-28. The room, the Mossy layout, Old Lintel, loot, and the local save were already in the tree. This phase is the session around them: leave the hearth, run a Rift, come home with the drop and the Depth.

The hamlet in [`demo-mvp.md`](demo-mvp.md) replaced the SwiftUI well as the launch screen. Cold launch opens on the village. This file’s loop is otherwise still the rift session. The session gate is unsigned.

The spec still wins on rules. `hearthdelve-full-build.plan.md` still wins on order. This file is only the slice in progress.

## Playable loop

1. Cold launch opens on the hamlet. The well opens the gate. `-seed N` still jumps straight into a fight so the old room checks keep working.
2. The well opens the gate. Pick a Depth up to your best, Delve or Deep Rift. The gate shows the biome from the versioned table and a predicted band (Easy / Fair / Hard / Deadly).
3. Start runs 1–3 floors of the Mossy rooms, the meter, the lantern on a Deep Rift, and Old Lintel at 100%.
4. Death or a clear keeps the loot. A Deep Rift clear at your best Depth adds the lantern jump. A Delve, a death, or a clear below your best does not move Depth.
5. The way deeper is closed for a Deep Rift past Depth 25. A Delve at any reached Depth still starts. There is no paywall yet.
6. The well equips a piece. Weapon item level changes damage on the next Rift. Armor still adds hit points and armor rating. The result and the bag show the two arrows.

Floors past the Mossy band still use the Mossy rooms. The gate says so. The hamlet and a thin smith are in. Other biomes and the rest of the buildings stay later phases.

## Not this phase

Hearth buildings, idle, Paragon, audio playback, StoreKit, Game Center, iCloud, Glyphs, and a second combat engine.
